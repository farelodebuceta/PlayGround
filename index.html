HOW TO PLAY

01 // MOVE

WASD to strafe. Mouse to steer. Space to jump — press it again in the air to fire your boot thrusters. Shift sprints, C crouches.

02 // SHOOT

Left mouse fires, right mouse aims. Six weapons, six attitudes. R reloads. 1–6 or the scroll wheel swaps hardware.

03 // SURVIVE

Clear four waves in Cityscape. Medkits drop from wrecked robots. When the portal ignites, press E and take the fight to Venus.

04 // TALK

Press TAB to open comms. The other robots have opinions. Enter sends, Esc closes. Their trash talk is part of the experience.

TIP // Headshots deal double damage. The Dragunov sees through the entire arena. The RPG is also a movement tool, if you are brave and/or stupid.
